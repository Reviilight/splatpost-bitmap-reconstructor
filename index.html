Splatpost Bitmap Reconstructor

A web app that helps reconstruct the original, pixel-perfect bitmap from an automatically saved image of a post from Splatoon games.

Supported games:

  • Splatoon 2 (Nintendo Switch)
  • Splatoon 3 (Nintendo Switch /2)
